Find the culprit

measure the room twice — whatever changed is the device

Put the scanner within a metre of the device you suspect. Do not move it between the two measurements, or you will measure your own hand.

STEP 1

Turn the device off

Unplug the thing you suspect, wait a few seconds, then record what the room looks like without it.

not recorded
STEP 2

Turn it back on

Plug it back in, give it a moment to start up, then record the room again with it running.

not recorded
STEP 3

Read the difference

Anything that got louder between the two recordings is coming from that device. Nothing else can explain it.

Longer is more reliable, especially for a device that only transmits now and then.
